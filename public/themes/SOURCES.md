# Anime background sources

These 1344x768 anime background illustrations are used as local app skins:

- `twilight.png` - Anime sunset countryside
- `forest.png` - Anime country road under a summer sky
- `stars.png` - Anime countryside under a starry sky
- `neon.png` - Anime business district and skyscrapers at night
- `illustrated.svg` - Project-owned card illustration pattern for the illustrated skin

Source: copainter Learn Free Assets, "Bus Stop Background Illustrations"
https://blog.copainter.ai/tag/assets-en/

`illustrated.svg` was created for this project and is covered by the project
license. The remaining files are from copainter; review the copainter asset
license terms before redistributing or using them outside this project.
